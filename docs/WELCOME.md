Hello, and welcome! InkCards is a spaced-repetition flashcard app for the
Xteink X4/X3 pocket e-reader. Study vocabulary, capitals, or any deck you like
on e-ink, with the SM-2 algorithm and physical-button grading.

## Start here

- New to the project? Read the [README](../README.md) and the
  [architecture overview](../ARCHITECTURE.md).
- Want to make a deck? The [companion tool](../companion/README.md) converts
  Anki exports and CSV files, and there are three sample decks in
  [`decks/`](../decks).
- Curious how scheduling works? See
  [spaced repetition](SPACED_REPETITION.md) and the
  [button mapping](BUTTON_MAPPING.md).

## How to take part

- **Share a deck**: post it here so others can study it too. Please only share
  content you have the right to redistribute.
- **Ask a question**: open a Discussion. For confirmed bugs and concrete feature
  ideas, please use the issue templates instead.
- **Contribute code**: see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md). Anything that
  needs a real device to verify is tracked in
  [hardware testing](HARDWARE_TESTING.md), and helping tick those items off is
  hugely appreciated.

## Community
